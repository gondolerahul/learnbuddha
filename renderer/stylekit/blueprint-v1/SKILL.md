## Blueprint kit: engineering, machines and how things work
Draw like a technical drawing that comes alive.
- The subject is a machine or structure built from props and Tubes (gears, battery, bulb, magnet, wires, pipes);
  show the mechanism working (gears turn, current flows as `electron` Particles, fluid flows in Tubes).
- Label with Labels on leader lines like dimensions and callouts; one yellow annotation (accent) for the key part.
- Camera: a wide "sheet" shot, then push into the subassembly that does the work, then pull back.
- Everything is linework on blue; avoid Landscape and the Guide.
