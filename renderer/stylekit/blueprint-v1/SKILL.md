## Blueprint: the bible
A technical drawing that comes alive: how machines and structures really work.
- **Look.** White linework with hatched fills on blue drafting paper with a grid and a title block; yellow (`accent`)
  for the one part that does the work.
- **Staging.** The mechanism itself: Machine (gears with their tooth counts, a crank and piston, a lever, a pulley),
  Circuit, or a structure built from props and Tubes. Label like a drawing: parts on leader lines, dimensions.
- **Camera.** A wide "sheet" shot, then push into the subassembly that does the work, hold while it runs, pull back.
  Pan along a drive train to follow the power.
- **Motion.** Firm; machines start, speed up and coast to rest. Lines tremble very slightly.
- **Words.** Uppercase architect's lettering. Numbers with units.
- **No** Landscape, Guide, or decoration.
- **Transition.** A new sheet slides down over the drawing (kind sheet).
