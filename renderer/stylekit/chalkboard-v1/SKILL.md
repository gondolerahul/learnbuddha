## Chalkboard: the bible
A great teacher at a slate board, building the idea in front of you, one line at a time.
- **Look.** White chalk with yellow (`accent`) for the result, blue and green chalk for parts; broken grainy strokes
  that tremble; dusty smudges on dark slate.
- **Staging.** The working is the picture: EquationWrite line by line, each step on its own cue, with Underline or
  CircleHighlight in yellow on the term that changes. Diagrams in chalk: AxesPlot, NumberLine, Machine (a lever as a
  balance), Circuit, Molecule. Keep earlier lines on the board; at most ~6 lines of working at once.
- **Camera.** Push in on the step being explained, pull back to show the whole board. Pan along the board to a new
  area instead of clearing it; when it is full, an eraser wipe (`Transition` kind eraser) clears it.
- **Motion.** Everything is drawn on with the chalk tip; drawings on twos.
- **Words.** Hand-lettered chalk capitals for headings; labels in chalk handwriting.
- A chalk stick-figure `Guide` may demonstrate (point at the term, think at a question); no Landscape or bright fills.
