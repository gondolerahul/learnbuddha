## Chalkboard kit: maths, physics and problem solving
Teach like a great teacher at a board: build the idea in front of the learner, one line at a time.
- Derivations and worked problems are the picture: EquationWrite line by line, each step on its own cue,
  with Underline/CircleHighlight in yellow chalk (accent) on the term that changes. Leave earlier lines on the
  board; the camera pushes in on the step being explained, then pulls back to show the whole working.
- Diagrams are chalk drawings: AxesPlot, NumberLine, Arrow and props (ball, spring, magnet, atom…) for the physics.
- Use the board's coloured chalks: yellow (accent) for the key result, blue (indigo) and green (sage) for parts.
- No Landscape or Guide; no bright fills. Keep at most ~6 lines of working on the board at once.
