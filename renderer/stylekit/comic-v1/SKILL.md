## Comic kit: stories, events, economics and social topics for teens
Like a comic page come alive: bold ink, flat colour, halftone shading, animated on twos.
- Tell it through characters and objects doing things (person props, Journey, props with motion); Callouts act as
  speech or caption boxes; one punchline per scene.
- Snappy rhythm: change the framing often (wide, medium, close), each on the word it serves.
- Bold colour, few words per label.
