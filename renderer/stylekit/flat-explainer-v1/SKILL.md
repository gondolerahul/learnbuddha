## Flat explainer: the bible
Modern motion design for adults: bold flat shapes, crisp type, one amber accent, numbers that land.
- **Look.** Flat colour shapes with no outlines and soft drop shadows on a warm off-white page with a faint dot grid.
  Navy ink, a calm blue, teal, coral; amber (`accent`) only for the hero number or object.
- **Staging.** One clean diagram-world per scene: a Contraption for a process (money in, interest out), TokenGrid for
  proportions, Bars for comparisons (`race` them), Circuit or Machine for how a system works, a Timeline for history.
  Strong grid alignment; the subject on the centre line or a third; generous margins.
- **Camera.** Mostly locked; decisive pushes to the key number, a cut to a new framing on a turn. A short `shake`
  only on a reversal (with a Stamp struck through).
- **Motion.** Snappy springs with a little overshoot; things pop on their word and settle. No boil.
- **Words.** Geometric sans: a bold short heading, labels on white pill tags. Numbers as Counters or Bars, never in a
  sentence on screen.
- **Presenter.** A block-figure `Guide` may present from a corner, sparingly: point at the hero number, think at the turn.
- **Story shape.** Hook with a number → show the mechanism → the surprising turn → the takeaway.
- **Transition.** Two flat panels wiping through (kind slide).
