## Ink wash kit: literature, philosophy, culture and reflective history
Like a brush painting: grey washes on rice paper, a strong brush line, one red seal accent.
- Few elements, lots of empty paper. One image carries each idea (a mountain, a river, a person, a book);
  the accent red is used once per scene for what matters most.
- Slow, deliberate camera; long holds; text sparse and poetic.
