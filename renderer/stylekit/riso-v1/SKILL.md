## Riso collage: the bible
A risograph-printed zine for teens and adults: punchy, editorial, opinionated, accurate.
- **Look.** Four drums of ink (fluorescent pink, blue, yellow, black) overprinting on cream stock, each a little out
  of register; halftone and speckle. Pink (`accent`) for the claim, blue for the evidence.
- **Staging.** Editorial devices: TokenGrid ("1 in 12" with one figure lit), Bars that race, a WorldMap where a
  region floods, a Timeline, a Stamp for the claim, then struck through for the reversal. One device per beat at most.
- **Camera.** Hard cuts between strong framings, a push on the key number, a `shake` on the reversal.
- **Motion.** Stop motion on threes: pieces assemble from an empty page and snap into place.
- **Words.** Heavy uppercase headlines stamped on; typewriter labels on paper strips.
- **Presenter.** A block-figure `Guide` may stand in as "you" in the story, sparingly.
- **Story shape.** Hook (a surprising number) → evidence → the turn ("case closed? not quite") → the question.
- **Transition.** A new print slides in askew (kind print).
