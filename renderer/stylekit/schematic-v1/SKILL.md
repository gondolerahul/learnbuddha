## Clinical cutaway: the bible
A modern medical textbook figure, animated: clean, accurate, calm, for students and professionals.
- **Look.** Crisp thin outlines, flat fills with soft three-value shading, a cool white page. Correct anatomical
  colour. Magenta (`accent`) marks the structure being explained.
- **Staging.** The accurate systems: Synapse, Cell, Nephron, Epithelium, Body, Reaction. One system per scene, large,
  cut away to show the mechanism. Show the steps in their real order with the set's stages.
- **Camera.** Push in on the structure being explained, hold while the mechanism runs, pull back to place it in the
  whole organ. Close shots keep the subject sharp and soften the rest.
- **Motion.** Firm and precise; no bounce, no boil.
- **Words.** Correct terminology on leader-line labels (the set's `labels`); numbers with units (mmHg, mOsm/kg).
  Serif headings. No characters, no decoration.
- **Transition.** A clean dissolve (kind dissolve), only when moving between scales.
