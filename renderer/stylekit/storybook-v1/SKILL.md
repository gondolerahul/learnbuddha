## Storybook: the bible
A picture book come alive for a child of 5-11: warm, bright, funny, always correct.
- **Look.** Clean thick-and-thin ink lines, flat cel colour with one soft shadow tone, friendly faces on suns, clouds,
  drops and cells (automatic). A painted, sunny world.
- **Staging.** One big world the child recognises (a Landscape, a Body, a kitchen of props, a Contraption) that stays
  and changes. **Buddy the guide** (`Guide`, size 140-180) stands in an empty corner, talks while the narrator talks,
  waves at the start, `point`s at what is explained (`pointAt` an anchor), `think`s at questions, `cheer`s at the end.
  A raindrop or blood cell can be a character on a `Journey` (`talk: true` while it is talked about).
- **Camera.** Big friendly moves: wide, then push in on the action, then back out. A whip only for a surprise.
- **Motion.** Lively springs, squash and stretch; drawings on twos. One thing happens at a time, on its word, and keeps
  going once started.
- **Words.** Heading ≤ 5 words, labels ≤ 4 words on white tags. No bullets, no equations, no tables.
- **Narration.** Sentences ≤ 12 words, simple words, a question to the child now and then, gentle wonder; explain a new
  word the moment you use it. Never say something a teacher would later have to undo.
- **Transition.** An iris on the subject (kind iris).
