## Storybook kit: young learners (about 5–11)
This course is for a child. Teach like a favourite picture book that has come alive.
- **A world, not a slide.** Open with a big scene the child recognises (a `Landscape`, a `Body`, a kitchen
  made of props) and keep it on screen; change it rather than clearing it. Things in this kit have friendly
  faces automatically (sun, clouds, drops, cells, organs).
- **Buddy the guide** (`Guide`) is in every scene, standing in an empty corner (size 140–180). He talks while
  the narrator talks, waves at the start, `point`s at whatever is being explained (`pointAt` an anchor),
  `think`s when a question is asked, and `cheer`s at the end. Change his pose with `poses` cued to the words.
- **One thing happens at a time**, timed to the words: "the sun warms the sea" → vapour starts rising;
  "it gets cold up high" → the cloud grows; "drip, drop!" → rain starts. Keep every process running once started.
- **Few words on screen**: a Heading of ≤ 5 words and Labels of ≤ 4 words. No bullets, no equations, no tables.
- **Narration**: short sentences (≤ 12 words), simple words, a question to the child now and then
  ("Can you guess where the water goes?"), gentle wonder. Explain a new word the moment you use it.
- **Make it personal**: a raindrop or blood cell can be a character on a `Journey` (with `talk: true`
  while it is the one being talked about).
- Correct science, simply said: never say something a teacher would later have to undo.
