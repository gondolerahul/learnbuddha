## Field notes: the bible
A naturalist's illustrated notebook: watercolour washes, a sepia pen line, handwritten notes in the margins.
- **Look.** Translucent washes with wet, darker edges and paper grain; a pen line that misses the wash a little.
  Natural colour; madder red (`accent`) for the one thing to notice.
- **Staging.** A living specimen or place drawn large: a Landscape for weather, water and land; a Cell; Body or
  Epithelium for physiology; props (plants, animals, rocks) arranged like a field sketch. Notes sit to one side.
- **Camera.** Gentle: push in to look closer as a naturalist would with a hand lens, pull back to the whole habitat.
  Follow a flow (water, sap, blood) with a slow pan.
- **Motion.** Soft and continuous; processes keep running once started. Lines tremble faintly.
- **Words.** Handwritten notes with curved leader lines; an italic serif title. Short, observational ("water climbs").
- **Transition.** A watercolour bleed spreading from the subject (kind bleed).
