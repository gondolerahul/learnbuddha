## Zen ink: the bible
Brush and ink on warm rice paper, like a patient teacher painting the idea in front of you. Calm, spare, exact.
- **Look.** Sumi ink lines that swell and taper with a dry-brush tail; flat grey and earth washes; lots of untouched
  paper. ONE vermilion accent (`accent`) per shot, for the thing to remember: it is the seal on the painting.
- **Staging.** Asymmetric: the subject on a third, the empty side for words. Few elements, each drawn large. History
  lives on a Timeline the camera travels along; places on a WorldMap; ideas as a Contraption or a stage of 2-3 props.
- **Camera.** Slow pushes and pulls, long holds. Pans follow a line being drawn (a route, a timeline). No whips.
- **Motion.** Soft springs; things are drawn on rather than popped in. Lines boil gently.
- **Words.** Headings in a book serif, labels handwritten with thin leader lines. Short, quiet, exact.
- **Transition.** A brush sweep of fresh paper (`Transition` kind brush), rarely.
- **Avoid.** Bright colour fills, more than one accent, busy frames, bouncing.
